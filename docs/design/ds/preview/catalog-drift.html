The page ground is the largest of the four and the easiest to miss, because it is painted on the body rather than on any card. The product floor is a cool grey carrying a teal and a blue radial wash — against a design that calls the floor "a warm off-white, never cool grey" and rules out "cold blue tech imagery". Every card in the catalog is judged against a ground its own design system does not describe. Next to that: the blog title and the test title are different colours in the same grid, which no design decided; and every typographic judgement made on this catalog so far was made in a typeface the product does not load. All four resolve in the theme cut.
