Filled neutral chip at 600 weight; selecting moves it to the accent fill with an inset ring and a drop shadow — the submit treatment, arrived at by picking an answer.

Why the same type as the catalog choice. The expanded catalog card previews the first question of a test, and entering the test shows that same question again. If the control is a light choice in one place and a heavy CTA in the other, the seam shows at exactly the moment continuity matters most. One type, one variant.

Never: an arrow on the answer variant — inside the test, answering navigates nowhere, and the glyph would promise something the interaction does not do. No min-height beyond what the padding already gives (the box clears 44px on its own), and no shadow on hover.
