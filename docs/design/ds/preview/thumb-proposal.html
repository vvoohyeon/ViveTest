The imported candidates, dropped into the slot

Decision D imported seven illustrations from 825385f6 unmodified. They are 300 × 200 (3:2) and the slot is 16:6, so object-fit: cover discards 43.8% of their height at every card width. Below is what that actually looks like.

The subjects are not clipped — they are left airless. Measured at the 292px card: the slot is 258 × 96.75, the artwork scales to 258 × 172, and the composition's centred subject lands at 79.1px (the face) or 94.6px in a 96.75px box (the panels). Nothing is cut off; every scrap of margin is. These were drawn centred in a 3:2 field with generous air, and 16:6 has none to give. And their palette is a second problem. Read off the files: #5C8E78 sage, #8A7BB5 violet, #C4704A terracotta, #C9A24A gold and #4A6FA5 blue — five accent hues against a system with one — plus two neutral greys, #7A7A85 and #A6A6AE, which are the pre-D-01 cool values of --warm-600 and --warm-500 and are now stale against the warmed ramp as well. Re-tinting all seven to sage would fix the palette and leave the composition problem exactly where it is.

Proposal — draw for the format, not for the subject

A 16:6 letterbox is a horizontal field. It rewards composition across the width — rhythm, layering, a line travelling left to right — and punishes anything centred and round. So the proposal is not a re-crop; it is a set drawn native to the format, in the single sage family, where a card is told apart by its composition rather than by its hue.

cadence — rhythm, tempo, pacing
overlap — type, disposition, two poles
fork — decision, branching, gates
layers — values, priorities, ranking
signal — metrics, measurement, trend
column — blog, long-form reading
pending — unavailable, coming soon

Four rules, so a new one can be drawn without asking. ⑴ Native 640 × 240, never cropped from another aspect. ⑵ Ground is --canvas, or --surface-soft for the unavailable variant. ⑶ Ink is the sage family only — --sage-muted, --sage-soft, --sage, and --muted-soft for the pending state. No second hue, ever. ⑷ The composition runs across the width and never centres a single round subject. Distinctness comes from arrangement, which is why seven of these read apart at a glance while sharing one colour.

Status: proposal, not adopted. Nothing here is written into colors_and_type.css or referenced by a card specimen, and the imported assets/ files are untouched. Adoption means promoting these to assets/ and mapping variants to compositions, which is step 5 work on real content — the mapping above is by kind, deliberately, because the catalog's variants will change and its kinds will not.
