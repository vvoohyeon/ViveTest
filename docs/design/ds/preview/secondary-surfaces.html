The pattern all six were missing

Every one of these surfaces was the same unwritten treatment, repeated by copy: rounded-[18px] p-5, a 94% translucent panel fill, a 22px-blur 16%-alpha shadow, and no border. VIVE says the opposite — an opaque fill, a 1px hairline, and a whisper of shadow, with hierarchy built from surface tint and hairlines and shadow reserved for genuinely floating things. Three classes now carry that, and the difference between them is what they rest on rather than how important they are: .vt-panel sits on the page, .vt-floating sits over it, .vt-well sits inside it.

Nothing on this card declares a dark value. Both columns are the same markup and the same rules; the semantic layer supplies the difference. That is the test of whether the dark mapping is a remap or a second palette — if any of these surfaces had needed a [data-theme] branch of its own, it would have been the latter.
